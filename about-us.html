How it all started

In 2019, Enstead was born out of a pivotal realization by our founder, Eniola. As an experienced entrepreneur, Eniola had witnessed the persistent struggles businesses face in not only generating high-quality leads, but also juggling the day-to-day demands of operations. Recognizing a gap in the market, Eniola saw a way to transform these challenges into an opportunity.

Fueled by a passion to see businesses thrive, Eniola handpicked a team of industry professionals who shared a bold vision: to create a comprehensive solution that would redefine how businesses approach growth. Enstead set out to do more than just lead generation—it became a platform offering both cutting-edge lead generation and seamless virtual assistance, empowering businesses to focus on what they do best.
